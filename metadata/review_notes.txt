Slochetok is a session notebook for coaches and therapists. You keep a client roster on this device, run each visit on a 0-to-10 scale, write Outcome, Know-how, and Action, then Review to file the note. It is for people who work with named clients, give each one a desk code, and want the visit to stay on the device.
